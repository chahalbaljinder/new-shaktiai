# UI Wireframe Descriptions

## Header

*   **Branding**: Displays the "SHAKTI-AI" logo and the tagline "Your AI Support".
*   **User Welcome**: A prominent banner welcomes the user back with a personalized greeting, "Welcome back, Shakti!".
*   **Privacy-Focused Status**:
    *   **Weather Widget**: A subtle, fake weather display (e.g., "28°C Clear Sky") is integrated. This serves as a privacy feature, making the app appear as a standard utility at a glance to protect user discretion.
    *   **System Status**: A clear indicator shows "All Systems Ready," assuring the user that the AI support is online and functional.

## Left Sidebar

A persistent vertical navigation bar provides access to all core features of the application.

*   **Primary Navigation**:
    *   **Dashboard**: The main landing page with an overview and quick actions.
    *   **Knowledge Base**: A repository of articles, guides, and information on various wellness topics.
    *   **Wishes Vault**: A private, secure space for users to save personal goals, reflections, or "wishes."
    *   **Settings**: To manage account preferences and app settings.
*   **Interactive Features**:
    *   **Voice Input**: A highlighted button to enable voice-based interaction with the AI agents.
    *   **Emergency**: A clearly marked "Emergency" button, providing immediate access to a pre-configured list of emergency contacts, hotlines, or support resources. This is the "PANIC icon" for critical situations.
*   **User & Display Settings**:
    *   **Dark Mode**: A toggle switch to change the UI theme for user comfort.
    *   **User Profile**: Displays the logged-in user's name and email, with a "Logout" option for secure session management.

## Main Content Area

This is the dynamic section of the application where the primary user interaction occurs.

### Dashboard View

This is the default view upon logging in, designed for quick orientation and engagement.

*   **Overview Cards**: Four prominent cards provide at-a-glance information about the service:
    *   **5 AI Experts**: Informs the user about the number of specialized agents available and includes a "Click to meet them" link.
    *   **24/7 Available**: Highlights the round-the-clock availability of the support service.
    *   **Private Secure**: Reassures the user about the privacy and security of their conversations.
    *   **Ready Voice**: Indicates that the voice interaction feature is enabled and ready for use.
*   **Quick Actions**: A set of three cards for initiating common tasks:
    *   **Ask Expert**: "Get instant guidance from AI specialists." A direct path to start a conversation with the AI team.
    *   **New Wish**: "Create and save your personal goals." A shortcut to the Wishes Vault feature.
    *   **Voice Chat**: "Talk directly with our AI team." An alternative entry point to the voice interaction feature.

### Meet Your AI Support Team View

This view is accessed from the dashboard and introduces the user to the specialized AI agents.

*   **Header**: "Meet Your AI Support Team" with a descriptive sub-header: "Five specialized experts ready to help with your wellness journey."
*   **Agent Profiles**: A scrollable list of individual AI agents, each presented in a separate card:
    *   **Maaya - For Moms, By Heart**: Specialized in pregnancy, childbirth, and baby care. She provides comforting guidance and factual advice.
    *   **Gynika - Flow Friend**: Focuses on menstruation, puberty, and contraception, offering straightforward information on reproductive health.
    *   **Meher - Gentle Guide**: Provides emotional support for trauma, anxiety, and abuse, creating a safe space for users to process their feelings.
    *   **Nyaya - Rights Ally**: An expert on Indian laws concerning consent, abortion, and family rights, helping users understand and navigate their legal options.
    *   **Vaanya - Age Rebel**: A health rebel dedicated to menopause, hormonal health, and women's empowerment, smashing taboos and providing strength.
*   **Agent Details**: Each agent card includes:
    *   An identifying icon and title.
    *   A short description of their specialty.
    *   A list of topic tags (e.g., "Pregnancy guidance," "Legal rights education").
    *   A "Chat Now" button to initiate a conversation directly with that agent.
